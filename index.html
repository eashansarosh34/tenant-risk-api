📊 Understanding the Calculator

Monthly Rent Amount

The monthly rent your tenant will pay. This forms the basis for calculating the total advance amount over the tenure period.

Tenant's Monthly Income

Used to calculate the rent-to-income ratio. Ideally, rent should be 30-40% of monthly income. Higher ratios indicate increased payment risk.

Credit Score

Credit scores above 750 indicate excellent creditworthiness. Scores below 650 may result in lower advance percentages due to higher default risk.

Rent Tenure

The length of the rental agreement in months. Longer tenures allow for larger total advances but also carry extended risk exposure.

Employment History

Longer employment duration indicates income stability. Tenants with 3+ years at current job pose lower risk.

Evictions & Late Payments

Past rental behavior is the strongest predictor of future performance. Any history of evictions or late payments significantly impacts advance eligibility.
